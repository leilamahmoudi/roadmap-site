Read `project-owner-expectations.md` and identify the specialist roles that would realistically be involved in building this product.

Do not use a fixed list of roles. Choose only the roles that are relevant to this specific project.

For each role, define:

* their lens
* their quality bar
* the decisions they own or influence
* what they would push back on

Keep the roles practical and avoid unnecessary roles or process.

Use these perspectives to establish concrete constraints and quality criteria for the next stages of the project.
